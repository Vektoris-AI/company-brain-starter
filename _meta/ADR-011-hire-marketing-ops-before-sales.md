# ADR-011: Hire marketing ops before a second salesperson

## Context
Ops was at 86% and we were delaying work; a second seller would sell what we can't deliver
([[2026-06-18-hiring-plan-meeting]]).

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| A: Marketing ops specialist first | Unblocks delivery | Sales stays one person |
| B: Second salesperson | More pipeline | Can't deliver it |
| C: Contractors | Flexible | Knowledge leaves with them |

## Decision
A, then an account manager if [[Cobaltline-Software]] renews. Decided by [[Dana-Kettlewell]] on 2026-06-24.

## Consequences
[[Zoe-Harper]] hired; [[hiring-plan-q4]] and [[q4-hiring]] track the rest.

## Revisit when
Pipeline coverage below 1x for two months ([[strategy-kpis]]).
