# ADR-015: Founders post; the company page reposts

## Context
Inbound conversations named founder posts, not the company page
([[founder-led-content-beats-company-pages]]).

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| A: Dana Tuesdays, Lena Thursdays, company page reposts | Personal, consistent | Founder time |
| B: Company page only | No founder time | Low reach |
| C: Ghostwritten for all staff | Volume | Feels fake |

## Decision
A. Decided by [[Dana-Kettlewell]] and [[Lena-Vasquez]] on 2026-08-26.

## Consequences
[[editorial-calendar-q4]], drafted via [[cmo-content-plan-routine]]; founders post themselves.

## Revisit when
Two quarters without a content-sourced conversation ([[marketing-kpis]]).
