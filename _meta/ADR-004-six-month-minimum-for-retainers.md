# ADR-004: Retainers have a six-month minimum term

## Context
9 of 27 retainers ended by month 4 ([[2026-03-03-retainer-churn-analysis-export]],
[[retainers-churn-in-month-four]]). Month 4 is when results start to show.

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| A: 6-month minimum | Time to show results, plannable capacity | Harder first sale |
| B: 3-month minimum | Easier to sell | Ends right before results |
| C: Month-to-month | Flexible | Churn stays |

## Decision
A for marketing ops and content retainers. Decided by [[Dana-Kettlewell]] on 2026-03-04.

## Consequences
Onboarding gets stronger ([[onboarding-sets-retainer-lifetime]]). Proposals state the term
([[proposal-sop]]).

## Revisit when
A client we want insists on a shorter term with a clear handover reason. Note: the
[[service-catalog]] now says 3 months, flagged there for review; see also
[[cobaltline-ops-retainer]].
