# About this example: Kettlewell & Finch is fictional

**Kettlewell & Finch is a fictional example company.** It does not exist. Every person, client,
partner, number, quote and event in this vault is invented to show what a working company brain
looks like after about nine months of use. Any resemblance to real companies or people is
coincidental.

## What the example shows
- A 23-person US B2B marketing-operations agency ([[company-profile]], [[org-chart]]).
- An AI leadership team of eight role agents working on the brain ([[ai-leadership-team]]).
- Projects linked to clients, people, decisions and sources (start at [[pipeline]] or
  [[client-health-scorecard]]).
- Decisions with revisit triggers ([[ADR-001-company-brain-single-source-of-truth]] onward).
- Two deliberately flagged contradictions, waiting for a human: [[service-catalog]] vs.
  [[ADR-004-six-month-minimum-for-retainers]], and [[pipeline-sprints-run-eight-weeks-in-practice]]
  vs. [[pipeline-sprint-six-week-scope]].
- The ingest trail from raw source to page: [[ingest-log]].
