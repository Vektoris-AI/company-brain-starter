# ADR-002: Focus on B2B services and SaaS with 20 to 200 employees

## Context
In 2025 we served 31 clients, many one-off and some consumer brands. Delivery never got repeatable
([[2026-01-07-founders-offsite-notes]]).

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| A: Niche to B2B services and SaaS, 20 to 200 employees | Repeatable offers, sharper message | Fewer leads |
| B: Niche by channel (paid media only) | Clear skill | Commodity pricing |
| C: Stay generalist | More leads | No repeatability |

## Decision
A, defined in [[icp-b2b-services-20-to-200-employees]]. Decided by [[Dana-Kettlewell]] on 2026-01-20.

## Consequences
[[positioning]] rewritten; consumer work ends ([[meadowquill-content-program]]); we say no to
brand-only work ([[Willowmere-Studio-Architects]]).

## Revisit when
Two consecutive quarters with fewer than 4 qualified opportunities from the niche ([[sales-kpis]]).
