# ADR-018: Stop selling paid media as a standalone service (proposed)

## Context
[[northmere-paid-media-pilot]] hit its cost target and produced the wrong leads; without tracking and
CRM work, paid media alone can't show pipeline ([[paid-media-needs-tracking-first]],
[[2026-09-30-northmere-escalation-call]]).

## Options

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| A: Paid media only inside a sprint or retainer | Tracking and CRM are in scope | Less revenue from small clients |
| B: Keep standalone, require tracking first | Keeps the offer | Still hard to show value |
| C: Drop paid media entirely | Focus | Loses [[Grace-Holloway]]'s strength |

## Decision
Proposed: A. **Not decided.** Owner: [[Dana-Kettlewell]], input from [[Marcus-Finch]] and Grace.

## Consequences
If accepted: [[service-catalog]] changes; [[Leo-Marsh]]'s contract hours shrink.

## Revisit when
After the Northmere relaunch (late October) shows whether tracking fixes lead quality.
