## What went wrong
Cost per lead hit the target ($93 in week 6), but only 6 sales-accepted leads in 6 weeks
([[2026-08-19-northmere-weekly-report-export]]). Leads were homeowners; the client wants
commercial property managers ([[2026-09-30-northmere-escalation-call]]).

Root cause: no conversion tracking from the CRM back to the ad platforms
([[paid-media-needs-tracking-first]]), and a brief that said "property owners".

## Agreed
Pause, fix tracking, relaunch commercial-only, no fee for the pause.
