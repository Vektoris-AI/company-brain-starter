# Role scorecards

A scorecard states the mission, 3 to 5 outcomes for the first year and the competencies.
Written before any job post ([[role-scorecards-before-job-posts]]).

## Marketing ops specialist (filled by [[Zoe-Harper]])
Mission: keep client CRMs clean and automations reliable. Outcomes: own two retainers' ops work
by month 6; zero unreviewed automations; document every workflow in the client's project page.

## Account manager (open)
Mission: be the single owner for 4 to 6 clients ([[one-owner-per-client-relationship]]).
Outcomes: Friday status for every client ([[weekly-status-report-sop]]); QBRs start with
client goals ([[qbrs-start-with-client-goals]]); no retainer ends before month 6.
