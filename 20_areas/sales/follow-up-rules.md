# Follow-up rules

1. Inbound lead: human reply within one business day ([[follow-up-within-48-hours]]).
2. After a discovery call: summary and next step within 48 hours.
3. Proposal sent: check-in after 3 business days, then 7, then close the loop politely.
4. Every follow-up has an owner from [[pipeline]]; agents draft, the owner sends
   ([[ADR-005-agents-draft-humans-send]]).
5. Long-time clients get a personal note, not a template (two agent drafts were binned for tone,
   [[2026-08-05-ai-leadership-pilot-notes]]).

Voice: [[brand-voice]]. Used by [[sales-pipeline-review-routine]].
