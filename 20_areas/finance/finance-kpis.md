# Finance KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Source | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash balance | Bank balance with as-of date | bank (read-only) | [[cfo-cash-check-routine]] |
| Runway (weeks) | Balance / avg weekly outflow, 8 weeks | bank | [[cash-runway-in-weeks]] |
| Receivable days | Weighted avg days outstanding | [[receivables]] | target < 35 |
| Gross margin per offer | Fee minus delivery cost | time tracker + invoices | [[service-catalog]] |
| Recurring share | Retainer revenue / total revenue | invoices | [[strategy-kpis]] |

Every number in reports carries source and date. Unknown = "not verifiable".
