# AI use policy

1. Only approved AI tools; approval needs a signed DPA, no training on our data, stated data
   location, deletion on request ([[vendor-dpa-checklist]], [[ADR-013-vendor-dpa-before-any-ai-tool]]).
2. Client data only in tools approved for that data class and allowed by the client's MSA
   ([[2026-07-15-pembury-legal-memo-ai-vendors]]).
3. Agents propose, humans decide and send ([[ADR-005-agents-draft-humans-send]]).
4. AI-drafted client content passes [[content-qa-sop]] like any other draft.
5. Disclose AI assistance to clients when asked; it's in the [[contract-playbook]].
