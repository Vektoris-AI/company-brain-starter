# Positioning

**For** B2B services and SaaS companies with 20 to 200 employees
([[icp-b2b-services-20-to-200-employees]]) **who** close deals but can't see or repeat where
pipeline comes from, **Kettlewell & Finch is** the marketing operations team that makes the CRM,
the automation and the reporting trustworthy, **unlike** ad-only or content-only agencies.
