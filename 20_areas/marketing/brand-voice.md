# Brand voice

- Plain and specific. A number or an example in every post; no "unlock", no "supercharge".
- We talk about the client's problem first ([[proposals-lead-with-the-problem]]).
- Honest about limits: "this takes 8 weeks if your IT is slow" beats a promise.
- Visual rules from [[rebrand-2025]]; designer [[Maya-Lindqvist]].
- Applies to client-facing emails drafted by agents too ([[follow-up-rules]]).
