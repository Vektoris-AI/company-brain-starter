> Draft by the CMO agent from [[editorial-calendar-q4]]. Dana edits and posts herself
> ([[ADR-015-founder-led-linkedin-content]]).

We stopped billing pipeline work by the hour this year.

Clients hated open-ended bills. We hated explaining them. So we wrote down exactly what a
pipeline setup includes, and what it doesn't, and put one price on it.

The honest part: our first two sprints took eight weeks, not six. Both times we waited on DNS
records. So now there's a technical checklist before kickoff.

Fixed scope only works if you're strict about the "not included" list.
