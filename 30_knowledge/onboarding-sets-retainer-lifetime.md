# Client onboarding sets how long a retainer lasts

Retainers with a kickoff workshop and a written 90-day plan lasted a median 14 months; without,
5 months ([[2026-03-03-retainer-churn-analysis-export]]).

**Practice:** every retainer starts with [[client-onboarding-sop]] and a weekly status
([[weekly-status-beats-monthly-reports]]). Early warnings go to the [[client-health-scorecard]].
