# Our best clients: B2B services and SaaS with 20 to 200 employees

Big enough to have a CRM and a sales team, small enough that no one owns marketing operations
full-time. Below 20 there is no budget; above 200 there is an in-house team and procurement.

**Evidence:** the 2025 recap ([[2026-01-07-founders-offsite-notes]]); current clients fit
(e.g. [[Alderbrook-Freightworks]], [[Cobaltline-Software]]). [[Stonemeade-Facilities-Group]] may
be too large.
