# What counts as a qualified opportunity

A deal counts when all four are true: a named decision maker was on a call, the problem is one we
solve ([[service-catalog]]), a budget range was mentioned, and a next step has a date.

**Why one definition:** marketing and sales numbers never matched at [[Cobaltline-Software]]
([[2026-01-14-cobaltline-discovery-call]]); same risk for us.
