# In practice, pipeline sprints run eight weeks

Two of two delivered sprints launched in week 8, not week 6. The pilot took 9.

**Evidence:**
- [[alderbrook-pipeline-sprint]]: two weeks lost to client IT (DNS records)
  ([[2026-08-27-pipeline-sprint-retro-alderbrook]]).
- [[kestrelmoor-website-leads-2025]]: 9 weeks, mostly approval waits
  ([[2026-02-06-pipeline-sprint-pilot-retro]]).

**Contradiction:** [[pipeline-sprint-six-week-scope]] and the [[service-catalog]] promise 6 weeks.
At 8 weeks the margin drops from ~41% to ~25% ([[2026-02-19-pricing-workshop-notes]]).

**Proposal from the retro:** plan 8, promise 6 only if the technical checklist is done before
kickoff. [[brackwater-pipeline-sprint]] is the first test. A human decides; flagged #needs-review.
