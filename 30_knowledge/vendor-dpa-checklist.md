# Checklist: what an AI vendor must sign before it sees client data

1. Signed data processing addendum (DPA).
2. No training on our or our clients' data.
3. Data location stated.
4. Deletion on request, with a time frame.
5. Listed in our approved tools with the data classes it may see ([[ai-use-policy]]).
