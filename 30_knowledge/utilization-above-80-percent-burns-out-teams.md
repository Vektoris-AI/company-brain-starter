# Utilization above 80 percent burns out delivery teams

At 86% the ops group worked weekends and survey agreement on "workload is sustainable" fell to 33%
([[2026-05-05-capacity-planning-meeting]], [[2026-05-28-employee-survey-summary]]). There is no
slack for client delays, sick days or learning.

**Target:** 70 to 80% ([[operations-kpis]]). After the slot rule: 78%
([[2026-10-01-q3-all-hands-notes]]).
