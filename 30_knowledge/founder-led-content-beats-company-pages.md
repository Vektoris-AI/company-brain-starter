# Founder-led content beats company pages

Buyers of a 25-person agency buy the people. Posts from a founder's own account get conversations;
the company page mostly gets the team's likes.

**Evidence (ours, small):** of the inbound conversations in H1, most named a post by
[[Dana-Kettlewell]] as the first touch; none named the company page. Confidence low until a
second quarter confirms it.
