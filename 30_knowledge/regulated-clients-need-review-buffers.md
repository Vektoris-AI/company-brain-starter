# Regulated clients need review buffers in the calendar

Financial-services and clinical reviewers take 5 to 10 business days. Plan it in, or every
deadline slips.

**Evidence:** [[Oakvane-Wealth-Advisors]] CCO review up to ten days
([[2026-04-22-oakvane-compliance-email]]); [[Wrenfield-Dental-Partners]] clinical review 3 to 5 days
([[2026-03-12-wrenfield-kickoff-call]]); [[Brackwater-Machine-Works]] engineers review technical copy.

**Practice:** [[ADR-009-legal-review-for-regulated-content]], [[content-qa-sop]],
[[oakvane-compliant-content]].
