# Cost per lead is not pipeline

A falling cost per lead can hide a falling share of useful leads. Measure sales-accepted
leads or meetings with target accounts instead.

**Evidence:** [[2026-08-19-northmere-weekly-report-export]] (cost down from $233 to $93, useful
leads flat). [[quillfield-abm-pilot]] measures meetings per account instead.
