# Plan capacity in sprint slots, not hours

Sprints need a team's attention in bursts; hour budgets hide that. A slot is "one sprint team
for 6 to 8 weeks". Sales can see free slots before promising a start date.

Source: [[2026-05-05-capacity-planning-meeting]]. Applied on the [[capacity-board]]; rule in
[[ADR-008-cap-concurrent-sprints-at-three]]. Retainers keep weekly hours.
