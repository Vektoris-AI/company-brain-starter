# A short weekly status beats a long monthly report

Five lines every Friday (done, next, blocked, decisions needed, risk) keep clients informed
and surface problems weeks earlier than a monthly deck.

**Evidence:** the clients who churned by month 4 mostly got monthly reports only
([[2026-03-03-retainer-churn-analysis-export]] notes).

**Practice:** [[weekly-status-report-sop]], measured in [[operations-kpis]]. Related:
[[onboarding-sets-retainer-lifetime]], [[cobaltline-ops-retainer]].
