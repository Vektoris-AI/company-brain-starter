# Proposals lead with the client's problem, in their words

The first page of a proposal quotes what the client said on the discovery call and what it
costs them. Our method comes second; price third.

**Why:** buyers forward proposals internally. A colleague who wasn't on the call needs the
problem first. Example: "We could sell more, but we can't onboard more"
([[2026-02-06-pipeline-sprint-pilot-retro]] context).

**Applied:** [[proposal-sop]], [[brand-voice]], [[sales-overview]].
