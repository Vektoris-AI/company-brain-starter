# A pipeline sprint fits in six weeks when scope is fixed

With one offer, one landing page, one email sequence and a CRM audit in week 1, a pipeline
setup can launch in six weeks.

**Basis:** the pilot plan and pricing math ([[2026-02-06-pipeline-sprint-pilot-retro]],
[[2026-02-19-pricing-workshop-notes]]): ~92 delivery hours.

**Conditions:** written "not included" list, 3-day client approval SLA ([[contract-playbook]]),
written handoff ([[sales-to-delivery-handoff]]).
