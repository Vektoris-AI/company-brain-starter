# Forecasts show two scenarios, never one number

A single forecast line looks precise and is always wrong. Show a conservative scenario (signed
work only, late payments, 8-week sprints) and a trend scenario (weighted pipeline).

Applied: [[forecast-model]], [[cfo-cash-check-routine]], [[2026-10-06-cfo-cash-check]]. Related:
