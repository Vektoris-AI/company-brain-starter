# Case studies need written client approval

No client name, logo, quote or number in public without written approval. Regulated clients
often can't approve at all ([[2026-04-22-oakvane-compliance-email]]: no testimonials).

**Consequence:** the [[website-relaunch-2026]] waits for approvals; ask early, at the
successful moment ([[alderbrook-pipeline-sprint]] 30-day report).
