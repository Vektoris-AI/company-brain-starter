# Upfront payment reduces receivable days

Half the fee before work starts means half the project can never become a late invoice.

**Evidence:** 49 days average outstanding in April ([[2026-04-01-ar-aging-export]]), 41 in
October after sprints moved to 50% upfront ([[2026-10-01-q3-all-hands-notes]]). Other changes
happened too, so the effect is not isolated.

**Decisions:** [[ADR-006-fifty-percent-upfront-for-sprints]], [[ADR-017-net-15-payment-terms]].
Tracked in [[receivables]].
