# Discovery calls reveal CRM debt the client doesn't see

When a client says their data is "fine", ask three questions: how many contacts have no owner,
how many lead sources exist, and who changed the routing last. The answers usually show debt.

**Evidence:** [[Granitefold-Insurance-Brokers]] had 25% of records without owner
([[2026-04-09-granitefold-crm-audit-export]]); [[Cobaltline-Software]] a third without lifecycle
stage ([[2026-01-14-cobaltline-discovery-call]]). [[Pinemarrow-Staffing-Group]] said "fine, I think"
([[2026-09-22-pinemarrow-discovery-call]]).
