# The sales-to-delivery handoff is written, not a call

A 10-minute call made delivery re-ask half the discovery questions
([[2026-02-06-pipeline-sprint-pilot-retro]]). A one-page handoff (goal in the client's words,
decision makers, scope and "not included", risks, approval SLA) fixes that.

**Template:** part of [[client-onboarding-sop]]. Checked in [[legal-contract-check-routine]]
(approval SLA) and used on [[pinemarrow-pipeline-sprint]].
