# Paid media needs conversion tracking before spend

Without a signal from the CRM back to the ad platforms, campaigns optimize for form fills, not
for the leads sales accepts.

**Evidence:** [[northmere-paid-media-pilot]] hit its cost-per-lead target and produced 6
sales-accepted leads in 6 weeks ([[2026-08-19-northmere-weekly-report-export]],
[[2026-09-30-northmere-escalation-call]]).

**What follows:** tracking is a precondition in the [[service-catalog]];
[[ADR-018-pause-paid-media-as-standalone-service]]. Related: [[cost-per-lead-is-not-pipeline]].
