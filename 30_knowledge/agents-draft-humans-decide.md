# Agents draft, humans decide

AI roles are most useful as tireless preparers: they read everything, compare it with the
rules and draft. The decision, the send button and the signature stay with a named person.

**Why:** the cost of a wrong email to a long-time client or a wrong payment is far higher than
the time saved. In the pilot, 2 of 6 follow-up drafts had the wrong tone
([[2026-08-05-ai-leadership-pilot-notes]]).
