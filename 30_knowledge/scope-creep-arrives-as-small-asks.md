# Scope creep arrives as small asks

"Could you also look at our newsletter" was said four times in one project
([[2026-02-06-pipeline-sprint-pilot-retro]]). Each ask is small; together they cost weeks and,
at [[Meadowquill-Senior-Living]], a disputed invoice ([[2026-04-01-ar-aging-export]]).

**Practice:** written "not included" list in every proposal ([[proposal-sop]]); log each ask
in the project page; the [[coo-delivery-check-routine]] flags them.
