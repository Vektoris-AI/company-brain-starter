# One reviewed source of truth comes before any AI tool

An AI assistant answers from whatever it reads. If that is a pile of outdated documents, it
answers confidently and wrong.

**Evidence:** [[Cobaltline-Software]]'s previous agency "got faster at being wrong"
([[2026-01-14-cobaltline-discovery-call]]); our own agents produced nonsense from a stale
[[pipeline]] page in week 1 ([[2026-08-05-ai-leadership-pilot-notes]]).

**What follows:** [[ADR-001-company-brain-single-source-of-truth]]; the agents in
[[ai-leadership-team]] read only the brain. Related: [[crm-hygiene-before-automation]].
