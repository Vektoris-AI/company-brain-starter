# Every client relationship has exactly one owner

One named person answers for the relationship: status, escalations, renewals. Others can talk to
the client, but the owner always knows.

**Why:** two bad handovers in Q4 2025 ([[2026-01-07-founders-offsite-notes]]).

**Applied:** owners in [[client-health-scorecard]] and [[pipeline]]; account manager scorecard in
[[role-scorecards]]. Agents respect the owner when drafting ([[follow-up-rules]]).
