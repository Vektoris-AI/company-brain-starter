# Every decision needs a revisit trigger

A decision without a "revisit when" never gets revisited, or gets revisited every week. The trigger
is concrete: a number, a date, an event.

**Applied:** every `_meta/ADR-*` ends with "Revisit when" (template in `40_resources/templates/adr.md`).
The [[ceo-weekly-review-routine]] lists ADRs whose trigger fired, e.g. [[ADR-003-productize-the-pipeline-sprint]]
after [[pipeline-sprints-run-eight-weeks-in-practice]].
