# SOP: client offboarding

1. Handover call: what we built, where it is documented, who owns it now.
2. Remove all our accounts from the client's systems the same day
   ([[client-data-least-privilege]], [[data-access-policy]]).
3. Delete any aggregated exports ([[ADR-007-client-data-stays-in-client-crm]]).
4. Final invoice and receipt check ([[invoice-run-sop]]).
5. Retro within a week, filed in the brain ([[retros-feed-the-brain]]).
6. Ask for a case study only if the result is real ([[case-studies-need-written-approval]]).
7. Move the project page to `90_archive/`.
