# SOP: Friday status note to clients

Five lines, every Friday, from the relationship owner ([[weekly-status-beats-monthly-reports]]):

1. Done this week
2. Next week
3. Blocked (and who can unblock it)
4. Decisions needed from the client
5. Risk, if any

Agents can draft it from the project page; the owner edits and sends. Missing notes show up in the
[[coo-delivery-check-routine]] and in [[operations-kpis]]. Used by [[Ben-Castillo]], [[Kevin-Ashford]].
