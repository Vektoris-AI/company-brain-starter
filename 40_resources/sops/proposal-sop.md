# SOP: writing a proposal

Owner: [[Jordan-Pike]]. Draft by the Head of Sales agent, human edits and sends
([[ADR-005-agents-draft-humans-send]]).

1. Page 1: the problem in the client's words ([[proposals-lead-with-the-problem]]).
2. Page 2: approach and timeline; start date only from the [[capacity-board]].
3. Scope and a written "not included" list ([[scope-creep-arrives-as-small-asks]]).
4. Price and terms only from the [[service-catalog]]; payment terms per [[contract-playbook]].
5. Check: retainer term vs. [[ADR-004-six-month-minimum-for-retainers]] (open contradiction, ask Dana).
6. Save the sent version as a raw source and log it ([[ingest-log]]).
