# SOP: content QA before a client sees it

Owner: [[Aisha-Morgan]]. Applies to human and AI drafts ([[ai-use-policy]]).

- Voice matches the client's guide (ours: [[brand-voice]]).
- Every number and claim has a source.
- No client names, quotes or results without written approval ([[case-studies-need-written-approval]]).
- **Regulated clients:** no performance promises, no testimonials, review buffer in the calendar
  ([[ADR-009-legal-review-for-regulated-content]], [[regulated-clients-need-review-buffers]]).
- Second pair of eyes for anything published under a client's name.
