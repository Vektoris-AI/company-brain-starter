# SOP: client onboarding (first two weeks)

Owner: relationship owner ([[one-owner-per-client-relationship]]), checked by [[Marcus-Finch]].
Why it matters: [[onboarding-sets-retainer-lifetime]].

1. **Day 0:** written handoff from sales ([[sales-to-delivery-handoff]]): goal in the client's words,
   decision makers, scope and "not included", approval SLA, risks.
2. **Day 1:** project page in `10_projects/<Client>/` from the project template; client page in
   `50_people/` updated.
3. **Day 1 to 3:** access requested in the client's systems ([[access-request-sop]]).
4. **Week 1:** kickoff workshop; 90-day plan for retainers, week plan for sprints.
5. **Week 1:** technical checklist for sprints (DNS, CRM admin, tracking), the main cause of
   [[pipeline-sprints-run-eight-weeks-in-practice]].
6. **Every Friday:** status note ([[weekly-status-report-sop]]).
7. **Day 14:** first health rating on the [[client-health-scorecard]].
