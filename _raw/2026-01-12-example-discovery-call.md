Discovery call notes, Example Customer Inc., 2026-01-12
Attendees: our founder; their Head of Operations

- They sell a B2B service and close new customers without much trouble.
- The pain starts after the contract: every new customer goes through a manual
  onboarding that lives in one person's head.
- "We could sell more, but we can't onboard more." (Head of Operations)
- Information is spread across email threads, a shared drive and chat.
  New team members ask the same questions every week.
- They tried a chatbot on top of their shared drive. It gave confident answers
  from outdated documents, so the team stopped trusting it.
- Interest: a single place where the current, reviewed version of each process
  lives, which both people and AI tools can read.
- Next step: we send a short proposal for a two-week pilot.
