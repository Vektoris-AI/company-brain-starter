Retro: pipeline setup pilot (Kestrelmoor Cleaning Co., Q4 2025), held 2026-02-06
Facilitator: Isabel Cruz. Present: Marcus Finch, Eli Brandt, Aisha Morgan, Jordan Pike

What worked
- Week 1 CRM audit found the real problem (no lead owner) within two days.
- One offer, one landing page, one email sequence: simple enough to launch.

What didn't
- Planned 6 weeks, took 9. Waited 2.5 weeks for client approvals on copy.
- Scope grew: "could you also look at our newsletter" was said four times.
- The handover from sales to delivery was a 10-minute call; we re-asked half
  the discovery questions.

Proposals
- Fixed scope, written list of what is NOT included.
- Client approval SLA in the contract: 3 business days.
- A written sales-to-delivery handoff before kickoff.
